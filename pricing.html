Which licence makes sense

The perpetual fee equals a little over five years of the subscription. That sounds like a clear win for buying once, but the perpetual licence freezes you on the version you bought unless you pay for updates later. New machines, fixes for new Windows builds and graphics changes all arrive through updates.

The annual plan suits schools that run a course each year, anyone who wants the newest machines and fixes, and buyers who are not sure they will still use the program in two years. The perpetual licence suits a shop that wants one stable version on an offline workstation and does not care about new features.

If you are not sure yet, run the free trial on the PC you will actually use. If you need something cheaper or free, the alternatives page lists options.

Refunds and cancelling

The order page mentions a 30 day money back guarantee on yearly licences. The pages checked do not describe a refund for the perpetual licence, so treat that purchase as final unless the vendor confirms otherwise in writing before you pay.

The pages checked also do not say whether the annual subscription renews automatically or how to cancel a renewal. Ask through the support portal before buying, and keep the order confirmation email, since it holds your licence details.

What happens after you pay

The vendor's help desk describes delivery by email after the order. You then open the program, choose Help, then Activate License, and paste the code into the License string field. Your account page on the vendor's site keeps the licence on record.

Install and test before buying: the download page has the official installer and setup steps, and the trial uses the same installer, so nothing has to be reinstalled when you activate.

Schools and multiple seats

Training centres are one of the main audiences, but the pages checked on 10 November 2026 do not list classroom, site or volume prices. Ask through the support portal for a quote before ordering single licences for a whole lab. The review covers the free training material that comes with every licence.

Frequently asked questions

How much does CNC Simulator Pro cost?

The vendor's help desk lists USD 99 a year for the annual subscription and USD 535 once for the perpetual licence. Check the official order page before buying, because the perpetual terms shown there have differed from the help desk.

Does the perpetual licence include updates?

Not for free, according to the help desk. You keep the version you bought; newer versions mean paying again. The annual subscription includes all updates during the year.

Is there a refund?

The order page mentions a 30 day money back guarantee on yearly licences. No refund terms for the perpetual licence were found on the pages checked, so ask the vendor first.

Is there a student or school discount?

None is published on the pages checked. Schools should contact the vendor through the support portal for multi seat prices before ordering.

How do I enter my licence?

Open the program, choose Help, then Activate License, and paste the code from your order email into the License string field. The trial installation stays in place, so no reinstall is needed.
